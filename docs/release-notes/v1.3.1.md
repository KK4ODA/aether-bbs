**See who Aether HF is hearing, and how well, right on the BBS dashboard.**

## Aether HF stations heard

- The dashboard has a new **Aether HF — Stations Heard** table, under the instance cards.
  For each station Aether HF has heard it shows:
  - the last **SNR** and the **best** SNR
  - when it was last heard ("2 min ago")
  - what it was doing (beacon, calling, probing, answering, session, KISS frame) and whom
    it was calling
  - its dial frequency, its bandwidth, and how many times it was heard
- It updates about every 10 seconds. Click a column to sort by it.
- Hover over a callsign to see when the station was first heard and whether you've had a
  session with it.
- Right-click a station to **Probe** it. The answer shows on that instance's card.
- With two Aether HF instances, an *Instance* column says which one heard each station.
- The table appears when an Aether HF instance has *Show Aether HF status* turned on, which is
  the default.

## Fixes

- After you saved Settings, the instance cards on the dashboard moved below the heard tables.
  They now stay at the top.

## Updating

From 1.3.0, Aether BBS offers this update when it starts. Click **Update now**.
